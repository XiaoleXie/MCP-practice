State Department Expands "Online Presence Review" to TN, TD, and I Visa Applicants

国务院将"网络形象审查"扩大至TN、TD及I类签证申请人

English

Effective October 1, 2026, the State Department is expanding its "online presence review" — enhanced social-media vetting conducted as part of visa adjudication — to cover TN and TD (USMCA professional and dependent) applicants and I (foreign media representative) applicants. Affected applicants are instructed to set the privacy settings on all of their social media accounts to "public" so consular officers can review their online activity.
